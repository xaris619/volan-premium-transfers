Private transfers from Larnaca Airport

Larnaca International Airport (IATA: LCA) is the main gateway to Cyprus and the closest airport to Ayia Napa, Protaras, Nicosia and Larnaca's beaches. Volan Premium Transfers runs private, door-to-door transfers from LCA around the clock – you never share the vehicle and never wait for other passengers.

Every booking includes flight tracking, so if your flight lands early or late your driver adjusts automatically. You'll be met inside the arrivals hall with a name sign, helped with your luggage and taken straight to your accommodation.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find my driver at Larnaca Airport?

Your driver waits in the arrivals hall of Larnaca International Airport holding a sign with your name. You also receive the driver's phone number on WhatsApp before you land.

What happens if my flight to Larnaca is delayed?

We track every flight, so your driver adjusts to the actual landing time. There is no extra charge for flight delays.

How long is the transfer from Larnaca Airport to Ayia Napa?

About 40–45 minutes for roughly 48 km, depending on traffic and your hotel's location.

How long is the transfer from Larnaca Airport to Limassol?

About 50–60 minutes for roughly 70 km via the A1/A5 motorway.

Do you provide child seats for airport transfers?

Yes. Baby seats, child seats and boosters are free of charge – just tell us the children's ages when you book.

How much is a taxi or private transfer from Larnaca Airport?

The price depends on the destination, number of passengers, luggage and time of day. Send your details on WhatsApp and we confirm a fixed price within minutes – the price you're quoted is the price you pay.
