Your own minibus and driver for the day

A private tour lets your family or group see the best of Cyprus without renting cars, navigating mountain roads or worrying about parking. Your driver picks you up from your hotel, follows the itinerary you choose and brings you back whenever you're ready. Tours are fully private and can start from Larnaca, Ayia Napa, Protaras, Limassol, Paphos or Nicosia.

Popular private tour ideas

Troodos mountains & villages

Mountain villages such as Omodos and Kakopetria, painted Byzantine churches, waterfalls and cooler air in summer.

Wine route

Visit family wineries in the Limassol wine villages and taste local varieties such as Xynisteri and Commandaria – everyone can taste, nobody has to drive.

Lefkara & Nicosia

The lace-making village of Lefkara, then Nicosia's old town, the Venetian walls and the Ledra Street crossing.

Kourion & Aphrodite's Rock

The ancient theatre of Kourion above the sea and the legendary birthplace of Aphrodite on the coast road to Paphos.

Cape Greco & the east coast

Sea caves, cliffs and beaches around Cape Greco, Konnos Bay and Ayia Napa.

Akamas & Latchi

The wild west of Cyprus – the Baths of Aphrodite, Latchi harbour and Polis.

Frequently asked questions

Can we create our own tour itinerary?

Yes. All our tours are private and fully custom. Tell us what you'd like to see and we'll suggest a route that fits the day.

Is the driver a licensed tour guide?

Our drivers are professional chauffeurs who know Cyprus well and can recommend stops. If you need an official licensed guide, ask us and we'll arrange one.

Are entrance fees and wine tastings included?

No, the price covers the vehicle, driver and fuel. Entrance tickets, tastings and meals are paid separately unless agreed in advance.

Where do private tours start?

We pick you up from your hotel or villa anywhere in Cyprus, including Ayia Napa, Protaras, Larnaca, Limassol, Paphos and Nicosia.
