Transport for any group size

Moving a group around Cyprus is much simpler with one vehicle and one driver than with a convoy of taxis. Volan Premium Transfers moves groups of any size: private minibuses with 8, 13, 17, 21 and 30 passengers, plus 55-seater buses, and several vehicles together for the biggest groups – from a single airport run to multi-day event transport.

What we transport

  • Weddings – guest shuttles between hotels, the ceremony and the reception venue
  • Conferences and corporate events – delegate transfers, team outings and incentive trips
  • Sports teams and clubs – training camps, matches and tournaments, with room for kit
  • Schools and organised groups – excursions and educational trips
  • Large families and friends – everyone arrives together with all the luggage
  • Airport group transfers from Larnaca and Paphos airports

Frequently asked questions

What is the largest group you can transport?

There is no limit. Our largest bus takes 55 passengers, and we combine several vehicles so any number of people can travel together.

Can you provide wedding guest shuttles in Cyprus?

Yes. We run shuttles between hotels, ceremony and reception venues, including late-night returns.

Do you provide airport transfers for groups?

Yes. We collect groups from Larnaca and Paphos airports with minibuses and buses for 8 to 55 passengers – and several vehicles for bigger groups – with room for luggage.

How far in advance should we book group transport?

As early as possible, especially for the larger minibuses in the summer season and for weddings. Short-notice requests are welcome – message us and we'll check availability.
