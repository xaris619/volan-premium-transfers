Larnaca Airport to Ayia Napa and Protaras

Ayia Napa and Protaras sit on the south-east coast of Cyprus, roughly 45–60 km from Larnaca International Airport. The drive runs along the A3 motorway and takes about 40–55 minutes. Paphos Airport is much further – around 190 km – so most visitors to this area fly into Larnaca.

Volan's private transfers pick you up in the arrivals hall and take you straight to your hotel, apartment or villa in Ayia Napa, Protaras, Pernera, Kapparis, Paralimni or Cape Greco.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the transfer from Larnaca Airport to Ayia Napa?

About 40–45 minutes for roughly 48 km via the A3 motorway.

How long is the transfer from Larnaca Airport to Protaras?

About 45–55 minutes for roughly 58 km, depending on traffic and your hotel's exact location.

Which airport is closest to Ayia Napa?

Larnaca International Airport (LCA) is closest – about 48 km. Paphos Airport is around 190 km away.

Can a group of 10 travel together to Ayia Napa?

Yes. We have minibuses for 8, 13, 17, 21 and 30 passengers, plus 55-seater buses, so the whole group arrives together with all the luggage.

Do you offer return transfers from Ayia Napa to Larnaca Airport?

Yes. We schedule the pickup from your hotel based on your flight time, 24 hours a day.
