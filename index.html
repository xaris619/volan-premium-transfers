FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much is a private transfer from Larnaca Airport to Ayia Napa?

The price depends on the number of passengers, luggage and time of day. Send your details on WhatsApp (+357 99 335570) and we confirm a fixed price within minutes – no meter and no hidden fees.

How long does it take from Larnaca Airport to Ayia Napa, Protaras or Limassol?

Approximately 40–45 minutes to Ayia Napa, 45–55 minutes to Protaras and 50–60 minutes to Limassol, depending on traffic. See the routes table for more journey times.

Do you provide child seats?

Yes. Baby seats, child seats and boosters are available free of charge on request for airport transfers and tours.

How big are your vehicles?

Private minibuses carry 1–14 passengers with luggage. For larger groups we provide coaches for 15–55 passengers.

Do you operate 24/7?

Yes. We run airport transfers, hotel pickups and private hire 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including early-morning and late-night flights.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

We track your flight and your driver adjusts to the real landing time, at no extra cost.

Where will the driver meet me?

Inside the arrivals hall at Larnaca or Paphos airport, holding a sign with your name. You receive the driver's contact details on WhatsApp before you land.

How do I book and pay?

Book via the quote form, WhatsApp, phone or email. No payment is needed to make a booking; we confirm the fixed price and all details with you in advance.

Which areas of Cyprus do you cover?

The whole island: Larnaca, Ayia Napa, Protaras, Paralimni, Nicosia, Limassol, Paphos, Coral Bay, Polis, Latchi and the Troodos mountains.
